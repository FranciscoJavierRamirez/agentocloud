## What data we collect

Only what you write in the form: name, work email, company and, if you wish, the system that concerns you. If you book a meeting, the scheduling tool receives your name, your email and the time you chose.

## What we use it for

To reply to you and arrange the conversation you asked for. We don't use it for anything else, we don't sell it and we don't share it with third parties, except with the providers that run the website, the form and the scheduling tool.

## Website measurement

We measure visits without cookies and without identifying people. The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

⚠️ Period to be defined. While there is an open conversation with you, and afterwards for the time required by law.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to contacto@agentocloud.com. We reply within one business day.
