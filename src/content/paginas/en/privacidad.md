## Who is responsible for your data

Francisco Ramírez Morales, founder of Agento Cloud, while the company is being incorporated. Contact: contacto@agentocloud.cl.

## What data we receive

Only what you give us when you contact us: your name, your email, your company and whatever you tell us in your message. If you book a meeting, the scheduling tool receives your name, your email and the time you chose. If you write to us on WhatsApp, we receive your number and your message.

## What we use it for

To reply to you and arrange the conversation you asked for. We don't use it for anything else and we don't sell it.

## Who we share it with

Only with the providers that run the website, email, scheduling and WhatsApp, and only for that purpose.

## Cookies

The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

For as long as our conversation with you lasts and up to 24 months after the last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to contacto@agentocloud.cl. We will reply within the period set by law. If you are not satisfied, you can turn to the personal data protection authority.

## Changes

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version here with its date.
