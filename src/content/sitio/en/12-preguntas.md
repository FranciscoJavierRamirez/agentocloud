preguntas:
  - pregunta: "Do I have to change my SCADA or my historian?"
    respuesta: "No. Agento reads what you already have, from any vendor."
  - pregunta: "Where does my data stay?"
    respuesta: "Wherever you decide: on your infrastructure, in a private cloud or in ours, with every access logged."
  - pregunta: "Does the AI make decisions about my plant?"
    respuesta: "No. Agento recommends and a person on your team approves. It doesn't write to the control system."
  - pregunta: "How do I know the diagnosis is right?"
    respuesta: "Every diagnosis shows its evidence and its confidence level. Models are adjusted only with approval, and there is a record of what changed and why."
  - pregunta: "Is it already working in a real operation?"
    respuesta: "We are looking for the first operation. We have two prototypes running on the same core, and the next step is to take them to a mine site."
  - pregunta: "Does it work for environmental monitoring?"
    respuesta: "Agento doesn't replace your monitoring system or report on your behalf. It warns you in time when an extraction or a level approaches its limit, says why, and keeps the evidence organized."
  - pregunta: "Are you an environmental monitoring company?"
    respuesta: "No. We are a twin of pumps, tanks and wells. Running them well is also the best way to stay within your commitments."
  - pregunta: "Is it only for mining?"
    respuesta: "We start with mining, but the same core works for other systems of pumps, tanks and wells: water utilities, desalination plants and energy."
  - pregunta: "How much does it cost?"
    respuesta: "It depends on the system. We start with a scoped diagnosis using your historical data; in 30 minutes we propose the scope."
